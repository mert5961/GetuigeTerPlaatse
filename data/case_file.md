# Case file: what the witness knows (fictional scenario)

Everything here is invented for a training demo. The witness may only state facts from this file.
Each line is one memory; the id in brackets is how answers cite it.

## Who she is
- [W01] My name is Anouk Visser, I am 67 and I live alone on the third floor, flat 3B, on the Kadoelenweg in Amsterdam-Noord.
- [W02] I have lived in this building for 22 years. I know most neighbours by face.
- [W03] The injured man is Daan Mulder from flat 3A, across the landing. He is about 40 and lives alone with a cat.

## What she heard and saw
- [W04] Around twenty to eleven at night I heard a loud bang, like a door hitting the wall, from across the landing.
- [W05] I looked through my peephole and saw a man coming out of flat 3A in a hurry.
- [W06] The man wore a dark green rain jacket with the hood up and carried a black backpack on one shoulder.
- [W07] He was tall, taller than the doorframe sign, and he walked with a slight limp on his left leg.
- [W08] He did not take the lift. He went down the back stairwell, the one that goes to the bicycle storage and the courtyard.
- [W09] I did not see his face clearly because of the hood. I think he had a short beard but I am not sure.
- [W10] A minute or two later I heard a moped start in the courtyard and drive away towards the Kadoelenweg.

## What she did
- [W11] I waited a moment, then opened my door and saw Daan's door standing half open.
- [W12] I pushed his door open with my right hand, on the outside handle, to look inside.
- [W13] Daan was sitting on the floor of his hallway holding his head. He was awake and talking but confused.
- [W14] I called 112 from my landline at 22:47. I remember because I looked at the kitchen clock.
- [W15] I picked up a phone that was lying on the floor next to Daan and put it on his hallway cabinet so nobody would step on it. I am not sure it was his phone.
- [W16] I did not go further than his hallway. I did not touch anything else.

## Things she only says if asked
- [W17] My doorbell has a small camera. My grandson installed it last year; it records when someone rings or moves in front of my door. I do not know how to look at the recordings.
- [W18] Not tonight but two days ago, in daylight, I saw a man in a green jacket standing in the courtyard looking up at the windows. I thought nothing of it then.
- [W19] Mr Bakker from the second floor came up the stairs after the ambulance arrived and walked into Daan's hallway to help. He touched the door frame.
- [W20] I wear reading glasses but not for distance. My distance eyesight is good.
- [W21] The light in the back stairwell has been broken for a week.
- [W22] I am shaken and my hands are cold, but I am not hurt.
